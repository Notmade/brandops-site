Coffee machine and accessories sourcing from across the globe

Retailers and cafe suppliers in India and the UAE often struggle to find reliable sources for coffee machines, grinders, accessories and spare parts at good prices.

BrandOps sources from manufacturers and distributors worldwide and shares options with landed prices, so you can choose the best deal.

What we source

  • Espresso machines and bean-to-cup machines
  • Grinders
  • Accessories and consumables
  • Parts and spares
  • Consumer tech and more on request

How it works

  1. You share the models or specs and the quantity.
  2. We come back with options and landed prices.
  3. We help verify suppliers and samples.
  4. We coordinate shipment, customs paperwork and delivery to India or the UAE.

Points to check before you buy

  • Voltage and plug type for your market
  • Certification and warranty terms
  • Spare parts and service availability
  • Import duty and VAT or GST on the landed cost
