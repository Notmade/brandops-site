GST registration for online sellers in India

Most marketplaces in India ask sellers for a GST registration (GSTIN) before they approve an account. Registering correctly, and then filing returns on time, protects your seller account and your payouts.

Below is a simple overview. GST rules and exemptions are updated often, so we confirm what applies to your case before we file.

Documents usually needed

  • PAN of the business or proprietor
  • Identity and address proof of the owner or directors
  • Business address proof
  • Bank account details
  • A digital signature for companies and LLPs

Returns you will deal with

  • GSTR-1 for outward supplies
  • GSTR-3B for the summary return and tax payment
  • Marketplace sellers also reconcile their sales reports with filings
