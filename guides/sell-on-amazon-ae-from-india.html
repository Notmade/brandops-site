How to sell on Amazon.ae from India

Amazon.ae is Amazon's marketplace for the UAE. Indian brands can reach UAE shoppers there, but the setup involves more than creating an account: the right selling entity, tax registration where it applies, a catalog written for the UAE market, and a plan for delivery and returns.

This guide walks through the path from first idea to live listings, and shows where a partner can take work off your plate.

What you need before you start

  • Company registration documents for the entity that will sell
  • A bank account that can receive marketplace payouts
  • A product catalog with clear images, titles and descriptions
  • Brand details, and brand registry where your category needs it
  • UAE VAT registration if your sales reach the legal threshold
  • A fulfilment and returns plan for UAE customers

The steps

  1. Choose the selling entity: an existing company or a new UAE company.
  2. Register the seller account and complete verification.
  3. Prepare the catalog in English, and in Arabic where it helps.
  4. Create and optimise listings so shoppers can find them.
  5. Set up fulfilment, shipping and returns.
  6. Grow organically with better content, reviews and ads.

How BrandOps helps

BrandOps handles company setup, UAE VAT registration and filing, seller account setup, catalog and listing content, and day-to-day account management, so you deal with one team instead of several vendors.
