Sell on Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio and Meesho

India's big marketplaces reach different shoppers. Myntra and Ajio lean toward fashion and lifestyle, Flipkart covers a wide range, and Meesho focuses on value and reseller-driven buying.

Each platform has its own onboarding, catalog rules and approval steps. Managing them together saves time and keeps your brand consistent.

What each platform needs from you

  • A GST registration and business documents
  • Brand and product details, with rules that vary by category
  • Catalog content that matches each platform's format
  • Pricing, stock and return handling that fit each marketplace

Where sellers get stuck

  • Account approvals and brand verification
  • Rejected listings because of image or content rules
  • Stock and order sync across platforms
  • Returns, payouts and reconciliation

How BrandOps helps

We run onboarding, catalog creation, listing optimisation, orders, returns and organic growth across Myntra, Flipkart, Ajio, Meesho and Amazon, through one point of contact.
