FAQ

Marketplace management, answered

What is marketplace management?

Marketplace management means we run your seller accounts end to end: onboarding and brand registry, catalogue and listing creation, keyword and SEO optimisation, A+ content, pricing, inventory and order ops, ads, reviews and account health.

Which marketplaces does BrandOps manage?

In India: Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio and Meesho. In the UAE: Amazon.ae and Noon. We can also run your own Shopify store alongside them.

How do you grow our sales organically?

We focus on search-first growth: keyword research, optimised titles, bullets and backend search terms, better images and A+ content, review and rating improvement, competitive pricing and in-stock reliability, so listings rank higher without depending only on paid ads.

Can you help us start selling in the UAE?

Yes. We handle UAE company setup, VAT registration, Noon and Amazon.ae seller onboarding, listings and fulfilment planning, so you can sell in India and UAE from one team.

Do you also handle GST, VAT and company registration?

Yes. GST filing, UAE VAT registration, Pvt Ltd and LLP formation in India, and free zone company setup in the UAE are part of BrandOps.
